class Global

package jta

@:directlyUsed

Holds the player's progress (coins, score, lives, completed levels) and handles save slots.

Each slot is its own FlxSave (slot1, slot2, ...), so erasing or corrupting one doesn't touch the others. Settings and keybinds are NOT stored here; those are shared across slots in Data.
